## Overview

This project documents aerodynamic analysis and flight
performance modeling conducted for the Hokie 0.75
spaceshot vehicle.

The work focuses on characterizing aerodynamic
performance, evaluating vehicle stability, and
supporting trajectory predictions through computational
and analytical methods.

## Engineering Objectives

- Characterize aerodynamic forces and moments.
- Evaluate aerodynamic performance across flight conditions.
- Assess vehicle static stability.
- Support trajectory modeling and performance predictions.
- Compare computational and analytical results.
